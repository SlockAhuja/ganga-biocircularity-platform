## 2. Operating & Capital Costs (OPEX / CAPEX)

### Operating Expenses (OPEX):
1. **Mechanical Harvesting & Collection**: ₹420 / tonne fresh biomass
2. **Logistics & Barge Transport (Prayagraj reach)**: ₹180 / tonne fresh biomass
3. **Bioreactor Digestion & Dewatering**: ₹310 / tonne fresh biomass
4. **Labor, Earthworm Beds & Packaging**: ₹140 / tonne fresh biomass
5. **Total OPEX**: ₹1,050 / tonne $\rightarrow$ **₹1,050,000 per 1,000 t**

### Capital Expenditures (CAPEX):
- 25 TPD Anaerobic Digester + Biogas Upgrader + 5 TPD Vermicompost Shed: ₹4.8 Crore
- Annualized CAPEX (15-year straight line at 8% WACC): ~₹56 Lakhs/year

## 3. Financial Metrics & Scenario Modeling

BioRiver models three scenarios:
- **Conservative**: Lower BMP (190 mL/g VS), ₹60/kg Bio-CNG $\rightarrow$ Net Margin: 18.5%, Payback: 5.8 yrs
- **Base (Current Baseline)**: BMP 245 mL/g VS, ₹72/kg Bio-CNG $\rightarrow$ Net Margin: 34.2%, Payback: 4.1 yrs
- **Optimistic**: Co-digestion with press mud, ₹78/kg Bio-CNG $\rightarrow$ Net Margin: 48.0%, Payback: 2.9 yrs
