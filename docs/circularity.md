# Circularity Score & Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)

## Circularity Score Index
The Ganga Biocircularity Platform uses a 5-pillar composite index:

$$\text{Circularity Score} = 0.25 S_{\text{harvest}} + 0.25 S_{\text{conversion}} + 0.20 S_{\text{nutrient}} + 0.15 S_{\text{waste}} + 0.15 S_{\text{energy}}$$

1. **Biomass Recovery Pillar ($25\%$)**: Ratio of harvested biomass vs accessible bloom extent.
2. **Resource Conversion Pillar ($25\%$)**: Percentage conversion of volatile fractions into methane and organic fertilizer.
3. **Nutrient Recovery Pillar ($20\%$)**: Plant-available NPK returned to local agricultural soils.
4. **Waste Diversion Pillar ($15\%$)**: Solid digestate and effluent diverted from river dumping or landfills.
5. **Energy Recovery Pillar ($15\%$)**: Net renewable energy output vs thermal baseline.

## Environmental LCA Factors
- **Riverbed Decay GHG Avoidance**: $64.2\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e / tonne fresh biomass}$
- **Fossil CNG Vehicle Displacement**: $2.75\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e / kg Bio-CNG}$
- **Grid Power Displacement**: $0.82\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e / kWh}$
- **River BOD Removal**: $185\text{ kg BOD / ha cleared}$
